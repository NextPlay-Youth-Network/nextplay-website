Give the Gift of Play

Your gift funds mentorship, sensory-friendly events, and real opportunities for youth in Lansing and the south suburbs.

Nextplay Youth Network is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

What Your Gift Does

Every dollar stays local and goes directly into programs for kids and teens.

$25

Supplies for one hands-on activity kit, from STEM projects to garden learning.

$50

A full month of mentorship sessions for one youth.

$100

Covers admission for youth at a sensory-friendly community event.

$250

Sponsors a peer mentor in our Bridge Division for a season.

Choose Where It Goes

Give where we need it most, or direct your gift to a specific division when you check out.
